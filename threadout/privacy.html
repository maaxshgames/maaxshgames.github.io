Threadout Privacy Policy

App: Threadout (Android) · Developer: MaaXSH Games · Effective date: 18 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game "Threadout" (the "app") uses, and why. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location. The app does not collect personal information for ourselves.

Game progress (levels, coins, hints, settings, daily rewards) is saved only on your device. We do not receive it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner, interstitial and rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. To show ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. This may be used to show ads that are relevant to you and to measure and prevent fraud. We do not control this data. Learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and policies.google.com/privacy.

In regions with consent laws (for example the EEA, UK and Switzerland) the app shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform before ads. You can change your choice later where the app offers it, or reset your advertising ID in your device's Settings.

If you buy the No-ads pass, the app no longer shows banner or pop-up ads.

3. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Coin packs and the No-ads pass are bought through Google Play. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We do not see or store your card or payment details. The app receives only confirmation that a purchase was made. A signed record of the No-ads pass is kept on your device so the game can unlock it, including offline.

4. Network and device permissions

  • Internet and network state: to show ads, check whether you are online, and complete purchases.
  • Vibration: to give feedback during play.

5. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help.

6. Data sharing and security

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with the services described above (Google AdMob and Google Play). Progress data stays on your device, and uninstalling the app removes it.

7. Your choices

  • Reset or limit your advertising ID in your device's Settings (Google → Ads).
  • Change ad consent where the app offers it.
  • Uninstall the app at any time to remove its local data.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be on this page, with its effective date.
